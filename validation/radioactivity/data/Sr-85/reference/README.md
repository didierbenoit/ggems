# Sr-85 selected reference

Selected authority: CEA/LNE-LNHB, PTB, 1998.

Apply delayed-level exclusion to the explicitly evaluated 514.007 keV
Rb-85 isomer (1.015 microseconds). Exclude its 514.0048 and 362.847 keV
photons and associated conversion electrons. The downstream 151.160 keV
photon/conversion totals mix prompt and isomer-fed relaxation and cannot
be split defensibly; exclude the undivided laws. This does not classify
all 151.160 keV emissions as delayed. Undivided compact atomic totals
are also excluded. No universal lifetime cutoff or timing change is used.

Retain the selected table/LARA 129.826, 354.97, 717.81 and 868.98 keV
prompt photons and their positive PenNuc conversion lines from directly
populated non-isomeric levels. The older commentary describes the
129.8 keV transition as tentative/upper-limit evidence, while the selected
table and compact files give 0.0005 percent; this weak fixed-law component
does not establish an experimentally confirmed intensity.

BetaShape's calculated ultra-small ground-state positron component is
not adopted by LNHB and has no retained spectrum; it is not included.
ENSDF gives 64.849 d versus selected 64.850 d. MIRD includes the delayed
dominant gamma law and therefore is not equivalent to this prompt subset.

Complete evidence remains in [raw/](../raw/). Unsupported Auger energy
laws, neutrinos, recoil and additional radioactive daughter decays are
excluded. See the [generic validation instructions](../../../README.md).
